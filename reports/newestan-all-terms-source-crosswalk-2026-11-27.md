## Findings and guardrails

- All 30 Newestan chapters have a source-linked Biyāmozīm record and a corresponding writing prompt.
- Terms 01 and 02 have developed model responses and a staged writing method for every chapter; Term 03 has chapter-specific teaching, topics and model responses, with N3-10 using its own research-project model.
- The Term 01/02 workshop presents teaching notes before the source exercise, subtopics, steps, rubric (only where source-supported) and model. Term 03 follows the same teaching-before-model principle.
- Term 02 genres are not collapsed into one essay template: email/letter, biography, illness report, historical report, film critique, memoir, recipe and formal request remain distinct.
- N3-10 is not presented as an ordinary essay: its research form, open main question, subquestions, source references, abstract and 5–8 keywords are preserved.
- OCR uncertainty is explicitly retained for N1-09, N3-02 and N3-07. N2-02's sports-registration form is noted as a separate source activity, not falsely described as the biography prompt.
- This crosswalk verifies source-file presence, chapter-to-record mapping, field completeness and the actual UI teaching order. It does not claim that OCR is error-free or replace human review of the original scanned page.
